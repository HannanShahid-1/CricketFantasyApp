# IPL Season Structure, Weekly Match Density, and Rules Relevant to a Weekly H2H Fantasy Format

Method note: All "COMPUTED" figures below were calculated by the researcher from the full fixture/result templates on the Wikipedia season pages (raw wikitext downloaded 2026-10-11 and parsed by script: date, IST start time, teams, round, result for every match). Sources: [2024 IPL – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Indian_Premier_League), [2025 IPL – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indian_Premier_League), [2026 IPL – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Indian_Premier_League). Wikipedia dates reflect matches as actually played (i.e., after rescheduling). Spot-checks against official iplt20.com fixtures were not possible in this session, so treat individual dates as Wikipedia-accurate rather than officially verified.

## 1. Season format, dates, and expansion plans

### Takeaway
IPL 2024, 2025 and 2026 all used 10 teams, 70 league matches (14 per team) plus 4 playoffs = 74 matches, over roughly 9.5 calendar weeks (65-74 days). The once-planned expansion to 84 matches (2025-26) and 94 (2027) did not happen through 2026; the IPL chairman now points to expansion with the next media-rights cycle from 2028, and no new teams.

## 2. Matches per team per week (fixture-list analysis)

### Takeaway
COMPUTED: In a Mon-Sun week, each IPL team almost always plays 1 or 2 league matches. 3-match weeks are rare (1, 4 and 5 team-weeks in 2024, 2025 and 2026). About 8 matches are played per full week, with double-headers almost only on Saturdays and Sundays. No week has had every team playing the same number of matches, so team-count imbalance (1 vs 2, sometimes 1 vs 3 or 0 vs 2) is built into any weekly format.

### Cited Findings (all COMPUTED from Wikipedia fixture templates)
- Distribution of team-week league-match counts (Mon-Sun weeks, all 10 teams x weeks with league games):
  - 2024 (9 league weeks, 90 team-weeks): 1 match = 41, 2 matches = 48, 3 matches = 1 (KKR, week of 29 Apr).
  - 2025 (11 league weeks because of the suspension, 110 team-weeks): 0 = 18, 1 = 48, 2 = 40, 3 = 4.
  - 2026 (9 league weeks, 90 team-weeks): 0 = 7, 1 = 31, 2 = 47, 3 = 5.
- Matches per Mon-Sun week (league): 2024: 5 (opening Fri-Sun), then 8, 8, 8, 8, 9, 8, 8, 8, then a playoff week with 4. 2025: 3, 8, 8, 10, 9, 8, 8, 3 (+1 voided), 3 (resumption weekend), 8, 2 (+3 playoffs), then the final alone in week 12. 2026: 2 (opening Sat-Sun), 9, 9, 9, 9, 8, 8, 8, 8, then 4 playoffs.
- Per-team weekly counts by season:
  - 2024: every team played exactly 1 in the opening week. In each later full week, between 1 and 2.
  - 2026: in the opening weekend only KKR, MI, RCB and SRH played. CSK played 3 in week 2. LSG played 0 in the week of 27 Apr.
- Number of "2-match weeks" per team (Mon-Sun): 2024: 5 for every team except KKR (4, plus one 3-match week). 2025: 3 (RCB) to 6 (LSG). 2026: 4 (KKR) to 6 (DC, LSG, RR). So a fantasy player's weekly opportunity varies by franchise.
- Double-headers, with the afternoon game at 15:30 IST and the evening game at 19:30 IST:
  - 2024: 11 (9 Sundays, 2 Saturdays).
  - 2025: 12 (8 Sun, 3 Sat, 1 Tue, 8 Apr).
  - 2026: 12 (8 Sun, 4 Sat).
  - All other matches start at 19:30 IST, except the 2024 opener at 20:00. No day had 3 or more matches.
- League matches by weekday: 2024: Sun 18, Sat 11, Fri 9, Mon-Thu 8 each. 2026: Sun 17, Sat 13, Mon-Fri 8 each. Effectively there is one evening match every weeknight and two on most weekend days.
- Days between a team's consecutive league matches: the minimum is 2 days, and no team played on consecutive days in any season. 2024: mostly 3-5 days (3 days = 30, 4 = 32, 5 = 37 cases), max 8. 2026: 3 days = 47, 4 = 31, 5 = 20, max 8. 2025: gaps up to 15 days because of the suspension.
- 2025 disruption detail:
  - Weather "no results": Match 44 PBKS v KKR (26 Apr) and Match 55 DC v SRH (5 May).
  - PBKS v DC at Dharamsala on 8 May was "abandoned and voided" and later replayed.
  - No matches 9-16 May (8 days). Play resumed Sat 17 May (Match 58 RCB v KKR, abandoned) with a Sunday double-header on 18 May.
  - The league ended Tue 27 May, and the playoffs ran Thu 29 May to Tue 3 June.
  - Weeks of 5 May and 12 May therefore had only 3 league matches each, and 4-5 teams played 0 matches in each.
- Abandoned or no-result league games: 2024 had 3 (Matches 63, 66, 70, all in the final week); 2026 had 1 (Match 12, KKR v PBKS, 6 Apr). Weekly scoring has to handle teams whose only match of the week is washed out.

### Inferences
- In a fixed Mon-Sun H2H format, roughly half of rostered players' teams play twice in a given week and half once. This mirrors NFL bye-week or NBA games-per-week management and can be a deliberate strategy layer (streaming 2-match teams). It is more variance than fantasy football's one game per team per week.
- Points per match are the fairer unit for leaderboards. For H2H, either accept the imbalance or use rounds that equalize appearances (see Section 4).
- Weekend double-headers make Sunday the busiest day. A Mon-Sun week ends with the most matches, so lineup decisions late in the week matter most.

### Gaps
- Fixture data was not cross-checked against official iplt20.com JSON feeds, and some exact start times could be off.
- Pre-2024 seasons were not analysed. 2022-23 used the same 74-match format, so results are likely similar.

## 3. Official IPL Fantasy (fantasy.iplt20.com) and other season-long cricket fantasy formats

### Takeaway
Official IPL Fantasy is a season-long, match-by-match game: an 11-player XI under a credit budget, a captain (2x) and vice-captain (1.5x), a season-wide pool of transfers usable before any match, and one-time boosters. It has no gameweeks: deadlines are per match. Big Bash fantasy is the main precedent for fixed rounds of multiple matches with per-round transfers and H2H leagues.

### Cited Findings

- Transfer allowance over time:

  - 2023: 110 transfers for Matches 1-56, unlimited for Qualifier 1, and 10 more for the remaining playoffs. Uncapped Indian players could be added for free, and transfers could be made between the two games of a double-header. — [Sportskeeda: IPL Fantasy League](https://www.sportskeeda.com/cricket/ipl-fantasy-league) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- The official IPL game's per-match deadlines and roughly 2 transfers per match (130-180 for about 70 matches) are tuned for daily re-optimisation, not weekly H2H. Weekly H2H differentiates itself by grouping matches and limiting moves per week, as the BBL classic game does.
- The Free Hit and Wildcard boosters exist because some match days leave a manager with few active players. In a weekly format, bench depth and weekly lineup locks would do the same job.

### Gaps

- No current (2025-26) rules were found for ESPNcricinfo fantasy, Fantrax cricket, or any Sleeper-style cricket app with weekly H2H. No major platform offering season-long weekly H2H IPL leagues was identified.

## 4. Candidate gameweek designs (fit within a ~9-10 week season)

### Takeaway
COMPUTED: Fixed Mon-Sun weeks give 8 full H2H weeks plus a partial opening weekend, with every team playing 1-2 times (rarely 0 or 3). Grouping by match number in 7-match rounds gives 10 rounds where every team plays exactly 1 or 2 times (2024 had zero exceptions). 10-match rounds give 7 rounds of mostly 2 matches per team. "Every team plays at least twice" windows give only 5-6 rounds of uneven length (8-15 days).

### Cited Findings (all COMPUTED from Wikipedia fixture templates)
- **Fixed Mon-Sun weeks:** 9 weeks with league games in 2024 and 2026, 11 in 2025.
  - Opening partial week: 5 matches in 2024 (Fri-Sun); 3 in 2025 (Sat-Sun); 2 in 2026 (Sat-Sun, with only 4 teams playing).
  - Then 8 full weeks of 8-10 matches each, then one playoff week (2024: Tue 21 to Sun 26 May; 2026: Tue 26 to Sun 31 May).
  - Not one week had all 10 teams playing the same number of matches.
- **Other week anchors** (league weeks, 2024 / 2025 / 2026):
  - Fri-Thu: 9 / 9 / 9 weeks.
  - Sat-Fri: 10 / 9 / 9 weeks.
  - Fri-Thu reduced 0-match team-weeks in 2025 (18 → 1) because it aligns with the Friday-Saturday restart. Otherwise all anchors give similar 1-vs-2 imbalance.
- **Rounds of 7 consecutive matches (10 rounds):**
  - 2024: every team played 1 or 2 in every round (60 one-match and 40 two-match team-rounds).
  - 2025 and 2026: 2 team-rounds of 0 and 1 of 3 each. Rounds lasted 5-7 days, except the 2025 round spanning the suspension (7-21 May).
- **Rounds of 10 consecutive matches (7 rounds, about 8-9 days each):** team-round counts 1 = 9-12, 2 = 46-52, 3 = 9-12. Every team has 1-3 matches in every round.
- **Rounds of 5 matches (14 rounds, about 3-4 days each):** most teams play once, but 14-19 team-rounds per season have 0 matches.
- **Greedy "every team has played at least 2 since last cut" windows:** 2024: 6 windows of 10-13 matches (8-11 days), with 1 match left over. 2025 and 2026: only 5 windows (11-15 matches), 8-9 matches left over, and some teams reaching 4 matches in a window.
- **Playoff week:** the 4 IPL playoff matches fit in one Mon-Sun week (2024, 2026) or span two (2025: Thu 29 May to Tue 3 Jun). Only 4 franchises play, and the two finalists play 2 matches each (or 3 for a team going Eliminator, Q2, Final).

### Inferences
- **Option A: fixed Mon-Sun weeks.** About 9 scoring weeks; merge the partial opening weekend into Week 1.
  - Pros: easy to understand (like NFL weeks), predictable weekly lock (for example, Monday 19:00 IST or per-player at toss), and it works with the 2025-style pause (skip a week).
  - Cons: 1-vs-2 (and occasional 0 or 3) match imbalance per franchise.
  - Fantasy playoffs: an 8-team league could run 6-7 regular-season H2H weeks (Weeks 1-7), with semifinals in Week 8 (last full league week) and the final in Week 9 (last league week). IPL playoff week is too thin (4 teams, 4 matches) for fantasy scoring unless rosters are re-drafted or it is played as a bonus "IPL Playoff Challenge".
- **Option B: match-number rounds of 7 (10 rounds).**
  - Pros: tightest balance (each team plays 1-2 times per round), and the number of rounds matches the 10-week feel.
  - Cons: rounds don't line up with calendar weeks (5-7 days), so lock times move around, and a suspension can stretch a round across weeks.
  - With 10 rounds: 7 regular-season rounds plus 3 fantasy-playoff rounds (quarter, semi, final), ending with the league stage.
- **Option C: 7 rounds of 10 matches (about 8-9 days).** Mostly 2 matches per team per round (about 72%), so it is closest to "every team plays twice" while staying simple. Only 7 H2H periods, which is short for a regular season plus playoffs (for example, 5 regular + 2 playoff).
- **Option D: "every team plays twice" windows.** Fairest per window, but only 5-6 per season with irregular lengths and leftover matches. Not recommended as the primary design.
- **Hybrid:** use Mon-Sun weeks but score each player's best N appearances, or average points per match, to reduce the 1-vs-2 imbalance. Alternatively, publish a per-week "matches this week" count for every player, as NBA fantasy apps do.
- **Expansion:** if the league moves to 84-94 matches (likely from 2028), the season would run about 10-11 weeks and the Mon-Sun format would gain 1-2 weeks.

### Gaps
- There are no published industry benchmarks for which design users prefer in cricket. These are researcher-proposed designs.

## 5. IPL rules affecting fantasy roster design

### Takeaway
Franchise squads have 18-25 players (at most 8 overseas), and at most 4 overseas can be in the XI. Since 2023, captains name their XI and 5 substitutes after the toss (about 30 minutes before the start), and one substitute can come in as an Impact Player. The rule is confirmed through 2027. As a result, 12 players per side can take part, but only 11 can bat. Fantasy lineup locks set before the toss can't rely on confirmed XIs.

### Cited Findings
- **Squad and overseas limits:** squads have a minimum of 18 and a maximum of 25 players, with at most 8 overseas. — [IPL player policies (iplt20.com)](https://www.iplt20.com/news/112745/ipl-player-policies-declared-for-2018-season); [Mumbai Indians: auction key rules](https://www.mumbaiindians.com/news/vivo-ipl-auction-key-rules-remember)
  - At most 4 overseas players in a playing XI. — [Wisden IPL 2023 explainer](https://www.wisden.com/series/indian-premier-league-2023/cricket-news/explained-ipl-impact-player-the-substitution-system-that-will-debut-in-ipl-2023)
  - These limits were not re-verified against the 2026 playing conditions document ([TATA IPL 2026 Match Playing Conditions PDF](https://documents.iplt20.com/bcci/documents/1774265031558_TATA_IPL_2026_Match_Playing_Conditions.pdf), not fetched).
- **Player Regulations 2025-27:**
  - "The Impact Player Regulation will continue for the 2025 to 2027 cycle."
  - Up to 6 retentions/RTM per franchise (at most 5 capped and 2 uncapped).
  - Auction purse INR 120 crore for 2025. Salary cap INR 146 / 151 / 157 crore for 2025 / 2026 / 2027.
  - INR 7.5 lakh match fee per playing member, including the Impact Player.
  - Capped Indian players become "uncapped" after 5 years without international cricket or a central contract.
  - Auction picks who withdraw before the season face a 2-season ban.
  - Source: [iplt20.com: Player Regulations 2025-27](https://www.iplt20.com/news/4109/ipl-governing-council-announces-tata-ipl-player-regulations-2025-27)
- **Impact Player rule (introduced 2023):** each side names an XI plus 5 substitutes, and one substitute can replace an XI member at any point. The replaced player takes no further part. If the XI already has 4 overseas players, the Impact Player must be Indian. — [Wisden](https://www.wisden.com/series/indian-premier-league-2023/cricket-news/explained-ipl-impact-player-the-substitution-system-that-will-debut-in-ipl-2023); [Scroll](https://scroll.in/field/1046219/impact-player-at-ipl-2023-all-you-need-to-know-about-the-new-rule-in-indian-premier-league)
  - In practice it makes matches 12 v 12, although only 11 can bat. — [ESPNcricinfo](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/35999996/ipl-2023-how-much-impact-impact-player-rule-have)
- **Team sheets after the toss:** since 2023, captains exchange teams after the toss rather than before, so the XI can be picked by batting or bowling first. — [WION](https://www.wionews.com/sports/ipl-2023-teams-to-name-playing-xi-after-toss-as-franchises-gear-up-for-upcoming-season-574714/amp); [cricket.com](https://www.cricket.com/news/teams-allowed-to-name-xi-after-toss-in-ipl-2023-report-3222023-1679483970229)
- **Impact Player usage example:** in 2025, RCB's regular captain Rajat Patidar was used as an Impact Player while Jitesh Sharma captained (23 May 2025). This shows that a star batter can feature only as an Impact Player, batting but not fielding. — [2025 IPL – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indian_Premier_League) (citing [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cricket/rcb-make-jitesh-sharma-captain-for-sunrisers-hyderabad-match-rajat-patidar-made-impact-player-heres-why-101748007789854.html))

### Inferences
- **Lineup lock:** XIs are only known after the toss (about 19:00 IST for 19:30 starts). A weekly lock (fixed day) means managers start players who may not play. Options:
  - per-player locks at each match's start, as in Big Bash;
  - automatic bench substitution for players not in the XI or Impact sub list;
  - a "played at all" rule that counts Impact Player appearances.
- **Impact sub scoring:** fantasy scoring must credit Impact Player appearances. Commonly, apps award starting-XI points only to the named XI and give the Impact sub "substitute" or appearance points. That rule detail was not verified in this research.
  - Batting-only Impact subs (the replaced bowler gone) produce batting-only scores.
  - Bowling-only Impact subs are common for teams bowling second.
- **Role volatility:** roles shift across matches because of Impact Player swaps.
  - Specialist batters may be subbed out after batting.
  - Keeper-batters may play as Impact batters while someone else keeps.
  - Part-time bowlers bowl less, since the Impact rule lets teams carry a 6th specialist bowler.
- **Roster size:** with 10 franchises x 25 = up to 250 players and roughly 120 regulars (12 per side per match), a 10-12-team H2H league with 15-16-man rosters is plausible. This is an estimate, not sourced.
- **Overseas limit:** a fantasy "max 4 overseas in lineup" rule mirrors the IPL rule. It was used by the official game (2019 rules: max 4 overseas).
